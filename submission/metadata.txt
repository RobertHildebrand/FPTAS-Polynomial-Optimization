Abstract (copied from the current manuscript; 69 whitespace-delimited words):
We give a short proof of a fully polynomial-time range-relative approximation scheme for polynomial optimization over the integer points of a bounded rational polyhedron in fixed dimension. The proof combines the reflecting-set decomposition of Cook, Hartmann, Kannan, and McDiarmid with Markov's polynomial derivative inequality and uses one integer linear feasibility query per refined cell. The running time is polynomial in the input encoding size, numerical degree, and reciprocal accuracy.
